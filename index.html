Toss: 0 open = 8 (ASHTO · અષ્ટો) · 1 = 1 · 2 = 2 · 3 = 3 · 4 = 4 (CHOKKO · ચોક્કો). ભગવાનના (Bhagwan-na): each player's first toss is dedicated to God and moves nothing. A toss of 8 also earns a 1-step પઘડી (PAGHADI), taken before or after the 8. A 4 or 8 may be accumulated (ભેગું કરો): each toss becomes an indivisible unit in the bank, and each unit (or a club of units) pays for ONE whole move. Three SAME-value 4s (or 8s) in a row burn the turn (બળી ગયા): the game rolls back to before the streak. Cowries wait on their home house (coloured ring); green crossed houses are safe for everyone.
